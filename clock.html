How to use

Enter names if you want, choose a mode and press Start. White moves first. After making a move on the board, tap your active clock to pass the turn. Only the active player’s time counts down.

Game time per player: each player has their own time allowance. “3 + 2” means three minutes each, plus two seconds added after each completed move.

Maximum time per move: the move allowance resets each turn. The optional game countdown covers both players combined and never resets on a turn switch. A game limit of 0 disables it.

Pause freezes both timers. Resume the correct player. Focus mode fills the screen and turns White’s panel to face the opposite player. Use Exit focus to return.

When time expires, the clock stops. Agree the result on the board, then press New game. This clock does not record results or connect to a club.

Keep this page open and your device unlocked. Wake lock is requested where supported. Switching apps is not a pause; your device can suspend the page. Closing or reloading loses the unfinished game. Settings are remembered where browser storage is available.

Use Download to save a self-contained HTML copy for offline use. On phones, opening local HTML depends on your browser/file app; the web link is the simplest option.
