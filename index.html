A QUICK GUIDE

Read me

How to use SEO Chess Club on your phone, tablet or computer.

Join the same club on any device

Open this app and enter the shared club passcode. Ask a club member for it. Everyone who uses the same passcode sees the same players, results and leaderboard. Use Copy app link to share the app; send the passcode separately. Remember me stores the passcode on this device, so use it only on a device you trust. Sign out clears it.

Add players and record results

  1. Open Players, enter a name and tap Add player. Names must be unique and 1–40 characters long.
  2. For a completed game, open Record result, choose two different players as White and Black, select the result and save.
  3. A win earns 1 point, a draw ½ point, and a loss 0 points. Ranking uses points, then wins, then fewer losses. Identical records are listed alphabetically.

Use the chess clock

Play on a physical board with one phone beside it. Choose White and Black, then a clock mode. Tap Start · White to move. After moving on the board, tap your active clock panel to start the other player’s turn. Fast duplicate taps are ignored.

  • Time for the whole game: the minutes are each player’s own allowance. Only the active player’s clock runs. An increment adds the selected seconds after every completed move. For example, 3 + 2 means 3 minutes each, plus 2 seconds per move.
  • Maximum time per move: each new turn starts with a fresh move allowance. Unused time is not carried over. Set Maximum game time to limit the total playing time for both players combined. The visible Game left countdown does not reset when turns switch. Enter 0 for no total game limit.

Pause, focus and finish

Focus mode gives the clock the full screen and turns White’s panel to face the opposite player. Exit focus returns to settings and navigation. Use Pause clock to stop all countdowns; resume the correct player to continue. Settings stay locked during a game. Pause before opening another tab, including this guide.

When either time limit expires, both clocks stop. Confirm White wins, Draw or Black wins to save the result. The app does not inspect the board or choose the result: a timeout can be a draw depending on the position and your agreed rules. Reaching the shared game limit does not automatically award a winner.

To finish before time expires, pause and choose the result. Once saved, use New game. Resetting or signing out during an unfinished game asks for confirmation and discards that game if accepted.

Correct mistakes and export

Open History to view results. Use Remove and confirm to delete an incorrect result, then enter the correct one. The leaderboard recalculates automatically. Export results downloads a CSV file for a spreadsheet.

Sharing, connection and device limits

Saved players and results are shared online and refresh approximately every 5 seconds while the app is open. If two people edit at the same time, the app refreshes the newer data and asks you to retry rather than overwrite it. If a save fails, check the message and retry. A failed save is not marked as saved.

Everyone with the passcode can add players, enter results and remove results. There are no separate administrator permissions. Keep the shared passcode within your club.

The running clock belongs to one device; it is not a remotely synchronised clock. Keep the page open and the device unlocked. The app requests a screen wake lock when supported, but your phone may still lock or suspend the page. Switching apps is not a pause. Reloading or closing the page loses the unfinished clock game; saved results remain online. An internet connection is required to join and save shared results.
